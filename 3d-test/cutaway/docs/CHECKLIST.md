# The checklist

The job description covers every animal. The checklist covers one picture. Write it before the first line of code: what does this picture stand on, in numbered lines a reader could check against it?

## Rules for writing one

- **10 to 12 lines.** Fewer misses features; more turns into a spec nobody grades.
- **Order:** silhouette and view first, then the signature feature, then materials, then the accent colour, then density.
- **Every vague word gets a number.** "Thin" became "0.15 to 0.25 of the leg's thickness" after thin bristles came back as quills.
- **Write "never" next to the default you are fighting.** "Clear glass, never a solid white shell." Without it the glass turns into one more white shell.
- **Proportions in numbers, measured off the picture.** Height, head and jaw as shares of the body length.
- **The last line is always density:** the dark share against the reference.

## Template

```
1. Silhouette and view: [the shape, what it stands or floats on, the hero angle]. Proportions measured off the picture: [height / length, head / length, limb or jaw / length].
2. The signature feature: [the one thing a viewer remembers: open jaws, a glass head, a raised tail]. [Size in numbers.]
3. [The second feature.] NEVER [the default that kills it].
4. Plates: separate curved plates in 3 sizes with gaps over the mechanism. Never one smooth shell.
5. Eyes: [size, lens, ring].
6. [Glass or open section]: what shows through it, in counts.
7. Limbs or fins: [how many, their pose, their membranes or bristles, with thickness in numbers].
8. Small parts: rivets, hoses, pistons in every gap.
9. Green only as accents: [exactly where]. No dots sprinkled over shells.
10. At 900 px wide it reads as dense as the approved still.
11. Dark share at least 0.8 of the reference's ([the reference's number]).
```

## Example: a robotic piranha, 12 lines

```
1. A deep-bodied piranha: body height 0.57 of its length without the tail fin, head 0.44 of the length, jaws (hinge to front) 0.36 of the length. A big forked tail fin on a narrow stalk. Front 3/4, slightly from above, floating over a soft floor shadow.
2. The mouth gapes wide: the opening at the front about as tall as the jaws are long (upper jaw up about 25°, lower down about 35°). Long curved glass fangs in 2 rows along both rims, the front ones about a quarter of the head height, tips glowing green.
3. A blunt front: the jaw ends rounded or squared, the lower jaw a little wider and ahead of the upper. NEVER a long low crocodile snout.
4. Head armour: separate curved white plates in 3 sizes with gaps, riveted. NEVER one smooth head shell.
5. A thick brow plate slanting down over the eye toward the snout.
6. The eye: a big glossy black lens with a thin green ring and 2 to 3 dark rings around it, under the brow.
7. The mid-body is a CLEAR glass barrel held by 5 to 6 curved white bands, with 2 to 3 big gears (0.3 to 0.4 of the body height) right behind the glass. NEVER a solid white side.
8. The tail stalk: 3 to 4 cylindrical segments, white collars, dark joints, thin green rings between them.
9. Fins: milky frosted membranes, thin dark rays, serrated edges glowing green; a dorsal fin of about 6 broad panels between spines; the tail fin as tall as the body.
10. The throat behind the fangs: a tunnel of rings lit green, visible between the jaws from the hero angle.
11. Green only as lines: plate edges, the eye ring, fin edges, coils, the gum line. Zero isolated green dots.
12. Dark share at least 0.8 of the reference's.
```

Lines 1 and 3 were added after round 2 came back as a crocodile with jaws at 0.52 of the body length. Round 3, with the numbers, landed at 0.35.
